About Crobot Technologies

Founded in Chennai in 2021, Crobot Technologies is a robotics and automation company dedicated to developing intelligent, real-world solutions for industries, education, and research. Our mission is to bridge the gap between people and technology by designing robots that empower businesses and inspire innovation.

Our Story

We started Crobot Technologies with a vision to create advanced robotics platforms that solve real-world challenges in automation, telepresence, and AI-powered navigation. Over the years, we have delivered cutting-edge products like UBOT, Ariyan, Romos with Hat, and Tulip—each tailored to meet specific needs in education, research, monitoring, and remote collaboration.

Our commitment to innovation, precision engineering, and seamless user experience allows us to design robots that are reliable, scalable, and easy to integrate into different environments.

Vision & Mission

Our vision is to make robotics accessible and impactful across industries, transforming the way people interact with technology. We aim to build robots that not only automate tasks but also enable smarter decision-making, safer workplaces, and improved communication worldwide.
