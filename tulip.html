Tulip

“ Building Strong Foundations in Robotics & Automation “

The Tulip Educational Robot is designed as a beginner-friendly, hands-on learning platform for students who are just starting their journey into robotics, AI, and automation. Unlike Ariyan, which focuses on industrial-grade training, Tulip is a compact learning kit that encourages students to assemble, program, and experiment—making learning fun and engaging.

Why Choose Tulip?

🤲

Students learn by assembling and operating the robot themselves.

🎓
