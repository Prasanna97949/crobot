Tulip

“ Building Strong Foundations in Robotics & Automation “

The Tulip Educational Robot is designed as a beginner-friendly, hands-on learning platform for students who are just starting their journey into robotics, AI, and automation. Unlike Ariyan, which focuses on industrial-grade training, Tulip is a compact learning kit that encourages students to assemble, program, and experiment—making learning fun and engaging.

Tulip Versions

Tulip is a fully open tinker kit robot designed for learning and experimentation. It is not industrial grade and supports additional sensor integration under specific conditions. It comes in three fixed versions—non-customizable.

Tulip Micro

Designed for university beginners exploring robotics fundamentals.

  • Motors: DC geared motors without encoder 12 V, & 5 V servo
  • Controller: STM32F407VET6 customizable board (ROMOS)
  • Supply Voltage: 12 V input from battery
  • Wheels: 85 mm rubber with plastic frame
  • Bluetooth: HC-05
  • IMU: MPU6050
  • Buzzer: 5 V
  • RGB LED: Custom board
  • IR Tracker: Line follower
  • Ultrasonic: SR04
  • OLED Display: 0.96 inch
  • Battery: 12 V 6 Ah lithium with charger
  • Charging Time: 4–5 hrs
  • Run Time: 3–4 hrs (based on speed/load)
  • Dimensions: Dia 30 cm, Height 14.5 cm
  • Weight: ~3 kg

Tulip Mini

Also for university beginners, with encoder motors for enhanced control.

  • Motors: DC geared motors with encoder 12 V, & 5 V servo
  • Controller: STM32F407VET6 customizable board (ROMOS)
  • Supply Voltage: 12 V input from battery
  • Wheels: 85 mm rubber with plastic frame
  • Bluetooth: HC-05
  • IMU: MPU6050
  • Buzzer: 5 V
  • RGB LED: Custom board
  • IR Tracker: Line follower
  • Ultrasonic: SR04
  • OLED Display: 0.96 inch
  • Battery: 12 V 6 Ah lithium with charger
  • Charging Time: 4–5 hrs
  • Run Time: 3–4 hrs (based on speed/load)
  • Dimensions: Dia 30 cm, Height 14.5 cm
  • Weight: ~3 kg

Tulip Mega

An intermediate-level robot with advanced features and Raspberry Pi integration.

  • Motors: DC geared motors with encoder 12 V
  • Controller: STM32F407VET6 customizable board (ROMOS)
  • SOC: Raspberry Pi 4 Model B (8 GB RAM, Ubuntu 22.02 with ROS2)
  • Camera: 5 MP with mic
  • LiDAR: 360° ROS scanner (6 m range)
  • Supply Voltage: 12 V input from battery
  • Wheels: 85 mm rubber with plastic frame
  • Bluetooth: HC-05
  • IMU: MPU6050
  • Speaker: 4 Ohm dual speaker
  • RGB LED: Custom board
  • IR Tracker: Line follower
  • OLED Display: 0.96 inch
  • Battery: 12 V 6 Ah lithium with charger
  • Charging Time: 4–5 hrs
  • Run Time: 2–3 hrs (based on speed/load)
  • Dimensions: Dia 30 cm, Height 14.5 cm
  • Weight: ~3 kg
