Ariyan

“Shaping Future-Ready Skills with Real-World Robotics”

The Ariyan Educational Series is a next-generation industrial-grade Autonomous Mobile Robot (AMR) designed exclusively for skill development, research, and advanced training. Unlike conventional educational robots, Ariyan brings real-world industrial standards into the classroom and lab, preparing learners for the future of automation and smart industries.

Why Choose Ariyan?

Industrial-Grade Design

Built with the same technology used in modern factories and warehouses, ensuring authentic, hands-on learning.

Skill Development Focus

Aligned with Skill India, Industry 4.0, and global education initiatives, Ariyan empowers students with job-ready skills in robotics, AI, and automation.

Modular & Customizable

From beginner-friendly configurations to advanced research setups, Ariyan can be tailored to match curriculum needs, lab facilities, and student levels.

Seamless Industry Transition

Trains students with industrial-grade hardware and software, bridging the gap between education and real-world applications.

Key Specifications

Jetson Orin Nano 8GB with 256GB SSD

360° LiDAR with 8m radius and depth camera

LFP Battery 24V with 3-4 hours runtime

Payload capacity: 10kg | Robot weight: 15kg

Ubuntu 22.04 & ROS 2 support

Dual speakers, mic, and IMU sensors

0.5 m/s maximum speed

Key Features
