UBOT

Welcome to a new era of seamless connectivity, where UBOT becomes your personal bridge to any environment—no matter where you are in the world. Designed to shatter barriers of distance, UBOT leverages real-time interaction, robust mobility, and a user-friendly web based interface to revolutionize the way you communicate and collaborate.

Key Features

Real-Time Remote Presence

Control UBOT from anywhere via a secure, web-based GUI.

Two-way audio and video streaming enables immersive, real-time interaction.

Intuitive Web-Based GUI

A customized, user-friendly dashboard for controlling UBOT’s movements, camera angles, and communication tools.

Minimal setup required—access from your laptop, tablet, or smartphone.

Versatile Mobility

Effortlessly navigate through various indoor environments such as hospitals, offices, and retail spaces.

Stable design for smooth movement and easy maneuverability in tight spaces.

Multi-Use Functionality

Built-in sensors and camera system for surveillance, consulting, remote monitoring, and more.

Adaptable to different industries—healthcare, education, security, corporate, and beyond.

Scalable & Secure

End-to-end encryption and secure data handling for sensitive interactions.

Easily integrate with existing communication systems for larger-scale deployments.
